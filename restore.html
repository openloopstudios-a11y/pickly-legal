Restore Your Purchase

Did you previously buy Pickly Pro? Here's how to get it back.

If you purchased Pickly Pro on a previous device or after reinstalling the app, you can restore your purchase for free using the steps below. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How to Restore

1

Open Pickly

Launch the Pickly app on your iPhone.

2

Go to Settings

Tap the ⚙️ settings icon on the Home screen.

3

Tap "Unlock Pro" or "Restore"

Open the Paywall screen and tap the Restore link at the bottom of the screen.

4

Confirm with Apple

Apple will verify your purchase. If found, Pro features will be unlocked instantly — no charge.

⚠️ Important: You must be signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase. If you used a different Apple ID, the purchase cannot be transferred.

Still Having Trouble?

If the restore doesn't work or you believe there's an error, please contact us and we'll help sort it out:

support@picklyapp.com

Refunds

All purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.
